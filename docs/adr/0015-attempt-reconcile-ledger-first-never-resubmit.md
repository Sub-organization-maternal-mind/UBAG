# Attempt reconcile: the ledger is read before a job runs, and a submitted attempt is resumed or failed closed, never run again

After a gateway crash or restart nothing renews the helper attempts it held, and the queue hands their jobs out again (a recovered file-spool lease, a TTL reclaim, a NATS redelivery). The attempt ledger knows what the primary saw; the helper knows what happened. Before P4.18 the only protection was that the next `BeginAttempt` refused a successor of a submitted attempt (`ErrAttemptSubmitted`), which failed the job closed even when the helper had finished it, and a job placed on no helper (the manager is down, so the picker grants nothing new) went to the local runner without ever reading the ledger: the prompt would have been sent a second time.

**The ledger is read before anything runs.** The worker consumer asks a reconciler about every leased job after the execution lease and before placement (`executor/reconcilegate.go`, behind `UBAG_HELPER_DISPATCH`; one ledger read for a job without attempts). The gate sits on the consumer, not on the file spool's startup recovery, because every queue backend ends as a lease there, and because returning a stranded job to the queue executes nothing. `RecoverOrphanLeases` is therefore unchanged: it still gives a stranded job back to the queue, and the gate decides what happens next. A ledger that cannot be read holds the job.

**The policy is pure** (`nodes.PlanReconcile`) and answers `run`, `wait`, `resume` or `fail_closed`. The ledger decides first; the helper is asked (`InspectAttempt`, read-only) only when the answer depends on it, and its answer can only refine the decision, never make a submitted attempt runnable.

- Unsubmitted, last lease still held or lapsed inside the margin (15 s, the helper's kill grace plus slack): wait. Once settled, ask the helper once: a helper that cannot answer, has no record or finished without submitting means `run`, and the next attempt takes generation + 1 (`BeginAttempt` supersedes the lapsed one and the old holder is fenced from then on). An unsubmitted attempt that the helper says did submit (the primary was lost after the click and before reading the event) is treated as submitted: the ledger is updated and the outcome collected.
- Submitted: ask the helper at once, lease valid or not. Still running or finished: resume. No record, or an answer for another generation or input, or a node revoked: fail closed immediately. Unreachable: wait inside a bounded window, fail closed after it. The window is counted from the lapse of the attempt's lease (nothing has renewed it since the primary was lost), default 10 minutes, `UBAG_HELPER_RECONCILE_WINDOW_SECONDS`. The helper stops an attempt at its own lease expiry, so waiting longer only helps to fetch an outcome it finished earlier.
- A closed fence (the attempt is expired or finished in the ledger) with a submitted attempt fails closed: nothing the helper holds can be committed.

**Resume never calls `RunAttempt`.** `RunAttempt` means admit-or-re-attach, and a helper that restarted would admit the attempt as a new run: the blind resubmit this slice exists to prevent. Resume uses only calls that cannot start anything: `InspectAttempt`, and `RenewAttempt` and `CancelAttempt`, which are fenced and answer NotFound for an attempt the helper does not hold. It takes the lease over on the ledger first and the helper second (the order of ADR-0014), polls `InspectAttempt` and, when the attempt has ended, commits its outcome as the attempt's terminal event through the same fenced `HelperIngest` as any streamed event (`ResumeAfter` is the last sequence minus one: the terminal is the last sequence). The ingest validates it like a streamed terminal; a failure after submission is `failed_terminal` with `reconcile_required`. The provisional events the primary never saw are not replayed: the result is complete, the event history has a gap. Resume tolerates a helper whose workload version or registry digest moved on (a deploy): the attempt's fence carries the version it was leased under, and a new placement still refuses such a helper. A helper that cannot be dialed when the window ends fails the job closed instead of holding it for ever.

**A stale result is rejected, twice.** An `InspectAttempt` answer is accepted only when its lease generation and input fingerprint equal the ledger's attempt and a finished answer carries an outcome and its last sequence (`HelperView.Consistent`). The commit goes through the fence, which rejects a stale generation on its own.

**The manager is not consulted.** The reconciler reads the ledger and the node store's last accepted endpoint and registry entry. A grant that lapsed because the manager stopped renewing it ends new placements, not the inspection of an attempt that was already placed (D3).

**The stale-job sweep stops expiring attempts when dispatch is on.** The reaper's `ExpireAttempts` closes the fence of every lapsed attempt, which would make a finished helper outcome impossible to commit. With dispatch on the reconciler owns the fence of a submitted attempt: it closes it by committing the terminal (the outcome or the failure). An unsubmitted lapsed attempt needs no expiry, because `BeginAttempt` supersedes it.

**Fail closed** is the existing ambiguous path (`ErrAmbiguous`): the job ends `failed_terminal` with `submitted` and `reconcile_required`, the event's `reason` is `attempt_reconcile_<reason>` (fixed tokens only), the queue lease fails and is never retried, and a lost or revoked node marks the conversations bound to it broken. Every decision is counted in `ubag_helper_reconcile_total{action,reason}` and a fail-closed one raises `UBAGHelperReconcileFailedClosed`.

Decided 2026-10-06. Residual risks, recorded rather than hidden: a helper that submits the prompt and is lost before any event reaches the primary, and that cannot be asked afterwards, leaves the ledger saying "not submitted", so the job is reassigned (the helper's `InspectAttempt` closes this whenever it can be reached; a durable helper journal would close it for good). A graceful gateway shutdown with a submitted attempt in flight still cancels the helper and fails the job closed (ADR-0014): only a crash or kill is resumed; detaching instead of cancelling is a follow-up. Turning `UBAG_HELPER_DISPATCH` off removes the gate, so drain before unsetting it (the runbook says so). Everything here is inert unless `UBAG_HELPER_DISPATCH` is on, and nothing is placed until a picker exists (P4.17).

Status: accepted
