# Helper grant shrink finishes in-flight work, ramp is 1-to-N on measured samples, pressure comes from the helper

A grant shrink or drain never evicts running work: the effective cap is max(new target, active usage), so nothing new starts until usage falls under the target, and the Drain RPC (stop new attempts, finish or cancel after grace) is called for `draining`/`revoked` grants with a 5 min default grace (capped at 1 h; a missing grace never means "cancel now"). Only non-voice attempts may be cancelled, and only after the grace has elapsed since a known drain start; voice calls are never terminated by UBAG policy and are left to end on their own. A new helper starts at 1 active workload and gains one workload per 8 consecutive healthy samples (CPU under 60%, memory available over 25%, pressure not reduced) taken while it ran at its current limit; any other sample restarts the run, and the earned limit is clamped to min(manager grant, ceiling table). Host pressure is reported by the helper agent (ReportCapacity cgroup use, optionally combined worst-of with host-wide readings); the primary never measures a helper, and an unusable report fails closed to a worst-case sample that can enter but never recover pressure. The manager grant stays authoritative for ceilings; telemetry only drives admission hysteresis. Decided 2026-10-06 because evicting work or jumping straight to the grant would turn a capacity change into user-visible failures, and measuring helpers from the primary would need a second trust path.
