# P4.18 Attempt reconciliation: helper lost, gateway restart, no blind resubmit

Status: merged when its PR is merged into `feat/perf-fleet`. Size L. Depends on P3.2 and P4.14 (both merged). Inert: everything is behind `UBAG_HELPER_DISPATCH` (default off), no migration, no public contract change. With the flag on and no picker wired (the placer is P4.17) every job still runs locally, but the gate below already protects a local run from a job whose earlier attempt submitted.

Design record: `docs/adr/0015-attempt-reconcile-ledger-first-never-resubmit.md`.

## What landed

- **`nodes/reconciler.go`** (the policy, a leaf: it imports only the job store's attempt types). `PlanReconcile` is a pure function from the job's ledger, the clock and optionally the helper's `InspectAttempt` view to one of `run`, `wait`, `resume` or `fail_closed` with a fixed reason. The ledger decides first; the helper is asked only when the answer depends on it (a two-pass plan: `NeedInspect`, then the answer). A submitted attempt is never `run`, whatever the helper says (`TestReconcileNeverRunsASubmittedAttempt` enumerates 3 states x 5 lapses x 3 inspect errors x 109 views). `HelperView.Consistent` is the stale-result rule (generation, input fingerprint, a finished answer carries an outcome and its last sequence). `Reconciler` reads the ledger, never dials a node the registry says is revoked, and asks an `Inspector`; the manager is never consulted. `ReconcileOutcomes` is the bounded label set of the metric.
- **`executor/reconcilegate.go`**: the ledger-first gate in `WorkerConsumer.RunOnce`, after the execution lease and before `Place`. `hold` (wait, or a ledger that cannot be read) is a delayed lease Retry, `resume` runs `Remote.Resume` under the usual supervisor (heartbeats, cancel watch) and closes the lease from the job's state like any remote run, `fail` is `*reconcileFailure` (`ErrAmbiguous`): the existing failed-closed path writes `failed_terminal` with `submitted` and `reconcile_required` and `reason = attempt_reconcile_<reason>`, fails the lease and never retries it. A lost or revoked node marks its conversations broken (`Manager.MarkNodeBroken`, the P4.16 follow-up). The picker is never asked for a job the gate resumes or fails.
- **`executor/remoteresume.go`**: `RemoteWorkerRunner.Inspect` (the `nodes.Inspector`: dial the ledger's node at its last accepted endpoint, one read-only call) and `Resume`. Resume **never calls `RunAttempt`** (admit-or-re-attach would start a new run on a helper that forgot the attempt). It adopts the lease (ledger first, then helper, under the attempt's own fence, workload version and fingerprint), polls `InspectAttempt`, and when the attempt has ended commits its outcome as the attempt's terminal event through the fenced `HelperIngest` (`ResumeAfter` = last sequence - 1). The helper saw the submission before the primary did: the boundary is recorded in the ledger first. No record, a stale answer, 120 s of silence or a lost lease fail the job closed; a closed window fails it closed when the helper still cannot be dialed.
- **`executor/remoterunner.go`**: `RemoteConfig.Nodes` (node id to endpoint) and `PollEvery`; the attempt carries its own `workload` (the fence uses the version the attempt was leased under); `incompatible(..., resume)` skips the workload and registry checks for a helper that already holds an attempt.
- **`helpermetrics`**: `ubag_helper_reconcile_total{action,reason}`, only the pairs the policy can produce, all present from the first scrape.
- **`serve`**: `newHelperReconcilerFromEnv`, `consumer.Reconcile`, `RemoteConfig.Nodes`, and with dispatch on the stale-job sweep no longer sets `reaper.Attempts` (the reconciler owns the fence of a submitted attempt; an unsubmitted lapsed attempt is superseded by `BeginAttempt`).
- **Ops**: ADR-0015, the runbook section "Attempt reconcile after a restart or helper loss" and a drain-before-unsetting warning, alert `UBAGHelperReconcileFailedClosed` (`deploy/prometheus/helper-plane-alerts.yaml`), ALLOCATION.md flag line.
- **`tests/chaos`**: three experiment definitions (`reconcile-gateway-lost-before-submit`, `reconcile-gateway-lost-after-submit`, `reconcile-gateway-and-helper-restarted`) and the stale-result and manager-down experiments now name the reconcile Go tests; `test_helper_dispatch.py` keeps all of them valid, reversible, labelled `definition_only` and tied to tests that exist, and pins the P4.18 exit-gate scenarios. They are NOT evidence of a live run.

## Flags and defaults

- `UBAG_HELPER_DISPATCH` (existing, default off): the reconciler exists exactly when dispatch does. Off: `newHelperReconcilerFromEnv` returns nil before reading anything and the consumer's gate is never set; the reaper wiring is the P4.3 one.
- `UBAG_HELPER_RECONCILE_WINDOW_SECONDS` (new, read only when dispatch is on): how long a submitted attempt may stay unresolved with its helper unreachable, counted from the lapse of its ledger lease. Default 600, 60..86400, else startup fails naming the variable.
- Inside the policy (not env): margin 15 s (the helper's 10 s kill grace plus slack), wait at most 10 s at a time, resume poll 2 s.

  - lost before submission: held while the lease is live, reassigned after the lapse, the new attempt takes generation 2 under a fresh id and the old holder is fenced (renew and commit refused);
  - lost after submission: a finished attempt is collected after a gateway restart (job completed, one terminal event stamped with the old attempt, attempt finished, no `RunAttempt`, picker never asked, local runner never run); a running attempt is adopted, renewed on both sides under its own fence and keeps living past its original lease on the real helper; a helper failure recorded while the gateway was down is handed back as `failed_terminal` + `reconcile_required`; a submission only the helper saw is recorded in the ledger first; a helper that forgot the attempt fails the job closed; the ledger itself refuses a successor in every case;
  - stale result: another generation, another input, a finished answer without outcome, and a helper turning stale mid-resume are rejected and nothing reaches the job (also on the real helper, with a lease generation that differs from the ledger's);

  - window: an unreachable helper holds the job and fails it closed exactly at the window (boundary +-1 s); a helper that cannot be dialed for the resume fails closed at the window instead of holding for ever; a ledger that cannot be read holds the job.

## Deviations

- **Files.** The work item names `nodes/reconciler.go`, `executor/filespool.go`, `serve/serve.go` and `nodes/reconciler_test.go`. `filespool.go` is NOT changed: the ledger is read where every queue backend ends (the consumer's gate), not in the file spool's startup recovery, because NATS redelivery and the TTL reclaim never pass through `RecoverOrphanLeases`, and because returning a stranded job to the queue executes nothing. `RecoverOrphanLeases` also runs inside `newDispatcherFromEnv` before any store exists. The effects live in `executor/reconcilegate.go` and `executor/remoteresume.go`, the dial in the existing runner.
- **No `attempt.submitted` event and no Python change.** The deliverable drops it: the existing `prompt_submitted` marker (P3.2) is the boundary the ledger records. The helper's own `submitted` flag in `InspectAttempt` closes the window where the primary died before reading the event.
- **Resume does not replay.** It commits the attempt's terminal outcome only (`InspectAttempt` carries the outcome, not the events); the provisional events between the crash and the end are not in the job's history. A streamed `RunAttempt` re-attach was rejected because it can admit a new run on a helper that lost the attempt.
- **The stale-job sweep** does not expire attempts when dispatch is on (a serve wiring change in `newStaleJobReaperFromEnv`; the executor's `StaleJobReaper.Attempts` field is untouched). Without it the sweep would close the fence before the reconciler could commit a finished outcome.
- **`MarkSubmitted` is not in the plan**: the resume derives it from the inspection it makes itself, so a plan can never carry a stale claim.
- Graceful shutdown with a submitted attempt in flight keeps the P4.14 behaviour (cancel the helper, fail closed); only a crash or kill is resumed (see follow-ups).

## Follow-ups

- **Detach on shutdown**: with Resume available, a graceful gateway shutdown after submission could leave the helper attempt running (no `CancelAttempt`, no `ErrAmbiguous`, lease Retry) so the next process resumes it instead of failing the job. It changes P4.14's tested shutdown semantics, so it is its own slice.
- **Drain before unsetting `UBAG_HELPER_DISPATCH`** is a runbook rule today. A ledger-only gate (no runner, fail closed on any submitted attempt) would make the unset safe without draining.
- **Durable helper journal**: a helper that submits and is lost before any event reaches the primary, and can never be asked again, is still reassigned. The helper's `InspectAttempt` closes the window whenever it can be reached.
- **A job with an open attempt and no queue entry** (the queue entry was lost or moved to failed) is not visited by the gate; the stale-job reaper times it out. P4.22 covers queued-in-DB-not-in-spool only.
- **Revocation of a node mid-attempt** is handled when the attempt is next reconciled (`node_revoked`, not dialed); stopping work on a revoked node while a runner holds the attempt belongs with the drain handling (P4.21 policy, not wired).
- **Postgres**: run the env-gated ledger suites (`pnpm test:gateway:postgres` or dispatched CI) before relying on the reconciler in production, and watch `ubag_helper_reconcile_total` and `UBAGHelperReconcileFailedClosed` from the first canary job (P4.20 is external-blocked).
