## Deviations
- The work item names `packages/proto/proto/ubag/v1/helper_voice.proto`; the slice deliverable and ALLOCATION.md require the `ubag.helper.v1` package, so the file is `ubag/helper/v1/helper_voice.proto`, as a second service in the same package rather than extra RPCs on `HelperService` (keeps `helper.pb.go` RPC surface byte-stable and `forbiddenRpcPattern` on `helper.proto`). The P2.3 follow-up "drop the forbidden-Voice check" is therefore intentionally not done: it still guards `helper.proto`.
- `voice_control.go` / the connect handler are not wired to the guard or minter: the helper dispatch plane (P4.x) that owns the lease and the helper client does not exist at this tip. The primitives are complete and tested; wiring is a follow-up.
- The reconcile of `feat/multimodal-voice-hardening` into main flagged as a risk in security-12 is moot: voice files are present at this tip.
- `packages/proto/gen/go/ubag/v1/jobs*.pb.go` regenerate differently (comment/SSE-resume additions from P2.7 were never regenerated); not touched here. Regenerating them is a separate follow-up.
- Stale writers are fenced by exact equality (`lease_generation` must equal the held lease), not just "older", so a forged future generation is refused too.
