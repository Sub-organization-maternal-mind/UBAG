# P4.11 ubag-helper service core against a fake runner

Status: merged when its PR is merged into `feat/perf-fleet`. Size L. Depends on P2.3 (helper proto), P3.10, P4.2, P4.3 and P4.5, all merged. Inert: a separate binary that nothing starts (not in compose, not in the gateway), and with no runner wired it refuses every attempt. It reads no `UBAG_*` feature flag; deploying the binary is the switch.

## What landed

- **Contract (first commit)**: `ubag.helper.v1` gained `HandshakeResponse.registry_digest = 7` and `ReportCapacityResponse.registry_digest = 13` (additive) and the `RunAttempt` comment now states the lifecycle (below). Regenerated with the pinned plugins (protoc-gen-go v1.36.6, protoc-gen-go-grpc v1.5.1, the versions in the checked-in headers); only the two helper files changed. `tools/check-proto-contracts.mjs` requires the field; `helper_test.go` pins its tags with golden bytes.
- **`apps/gateway/cmd/ubag-helper`** (new, ~60 lines): reads the environment, opens the node, serves until SIGINT/SIGTERM, then shuts down (running attempts end `failed`, `stream_end_reason` `shutdown`).
- **`apps/gateway/internal/helper`** (the service core; package `helper`):
  - `server.go`: `Server` implements `HelperServiceServer`. Handshake (protocol, helper/workload version, features, `server_time`, registry digest), ReportCapacity, RunAttempt, RenewAttempt, CancelAttempt, InspectAttempt, Drain. `StageManifest` answers `Unimplemented` (assets are pulled by the helper under attempt tokens, P4.10).
  - `attempts.go`: the attempt lifecycle. An attempt lives on its **lease, not its stream**: events go into a bounded per-attempt log (60 KiB per event, 4 MiB, 4096 events, the primary's ingest bounds, a test pins them equal), `RunAttempt` streams read the log, a broken stream loses nothing, and a repeated `RunAttempt` with the same job, attempt id, generation and fingerprint **re-attaches and replays from sequence 1** (the primary dedupes by event key). **Local self-expiry**: a timer kills an attempt whose lease is not renewed (runner context cancelled, terminal `failed` / `helper_lease_expired`); a renew never shortens a lease, is clamped to `now + 5 min` and cannot resurrect a lapsed one. A killed attempt whose runner ignores cancellation gets its terminal written after `KillGrace` (10 s) while its identity slot stays held until `Run` really returns. Deadline backstop: `deadline_seconds + 15 s` ends the attempt `timed_out` with `partial` set only if tokens streamed and never a result (D4). Output over a bound **fails the attempt** (`helper_output_limit`), never truncated; invalid events fail it (`helper_event_invalid`); a runner that panics, errors or returns without a terminal ends it `failed` with a sanitized fixed message (the runner's own text never leaves the helper). The helper's own knowledge of the submission boundary wins over the runner's claim: `submitted` is forced true after `PROMPT_SUBMITTED` and a failed outcome then gets `reconcile_required` (D4).
  - **Fencing**: Renew, Cancel and re-attach need the exact (job, attempt, generation, node) the helper holds; wrong node `PermissionDenied`, stale or foreign generation / attempt `Aborted`, unknown `NotFound`, fingerprint or workload mismatch `FailedPrecondition`. A new attempt on a job with a lower generation, or a second attempt id on the same generation, is `Aborted`; a higher generation **supersedes** the older holder (killed, `superseded`, and it can no longer renew, cancel or re-attach).
  - `pool.go`: admission bookkeeping, not a process pool: at most `UBAG_HELPER_MAX_ATTEMPTS` concurrent attempts (default 1, hard ceiling 8) and **one active operation per (provider, identity_ref)** (D6); over capacity, identity busy, draining, closed or no runner are `Unavailable`. Identity slots (BUSY / IDLE / MANUAL_ACTION_REQUIRED after an attempt that stopped on a manual-action event) are reported in ReportCapacity.
  - `validate.go`: field shapes and bounds (identifiers, JSON object input within 512 KiB, options within 64 KiB, at most 64 assets of at most 32 MiB with a sha256, deadline 0 = 25 min default and at most 1 h) and **`antigravity_*` refused** for target or provider, any case.
  - `Drain`: stops new attempts; running work finishes; attempts still running when the grace elapses are cancelled (`drain_grace_elapsed`); idempotent (the first call starts the clock); a missing grace defaults to 5 min, capped at 1 h (ADR-0009: never "cancel now").
  - `InspectAttempt` is read-only and unfenced: `UNKNOWN` (not an error) for an attempt this process has no record of (a restarted helper has no journal, so the primary reconciles from its own ledger); after a runner crash it returns FINISHED with the failed, `submitted`, `reconcile_required` outcome. Finished attempts are kept `Retain` (1 h, at most 256) and then forgotten with their per-job fence entry.
  - `runner.go`: the `Runner` interface (`Run(ctx, AttemptSpec, emit)`), documented contract; P4.12 plugs the warm-daemon pool in behind it.
  - `auth.go` / `keypair.go`: the helper side of the trust plane (ADR-0013). The helper authenticates the **primary** from its client certificate: TLS 1.3 only, client certificate required and chained to `UBAG_HELPER_CA_FILE`, exactly one URI SAN `spiffe://ubag/primary/<id>` equal to `UBAG_HELPER_PRIMARY_URI_SAN` (a Helper Node's `spiffe://ubag/node/*` certificate, also CA-issued and `clientAuth`-capable, can never act as the primary), `clientAuth` EKU, not a CA, at most 72 h (+10 min slack), optional SPKI pins (at most two). No session tickets, no reflection or health service, unknown services `Unimplemented` only after authentication, 1 MiB receive bound, 32 concurrent streams, keepalive that reaps half-open connections (clients must not ping more often than every 10 s). Interceptors re-verify every RPC and fail closed when wired to a connection with no verified peer; a stream's context ends when the peer's certificate expires (the attempt does not). The helper's own certificate is vetted at start and on every reload (exactly its node URI SAN, `serverAuth`, valid now, at most 72 h) and hot-reloaded when the files change.
  - `config.go`: `SettingsFromEnv` + `Settings.Open` + `Node.Run`. **Refuses to start** (all problems reported together, values never printed) when: the environment carries primary-only configuration (`UBAG_APP_SECRET`, database/DSN, MinIO/Garage, NATS, webhook/voice secrets, `UBAG_REMOTE_BROWSER_ENDPOINT`, `UBAG_NOVNC_BASE_URL`, `UBAG_FLEET_*`, `UBAG_ANTIGRAVITY_*`, any other `UBAG_*` ending `_SECRET`/`_TOKEN`/`_PASSWORD`/`_API_KEY`/`_DSN`/...; `UBAG_HELPER_*` is exempt); there is **no local Chrome** (`UBAG_HELPER_CHROME_BIN` or `google-chrome`, `chromium`, ... on PATH); `UBAG_HELPER_LISTEN` is not a single interface IP literal and port (no hostname, no wildcard); its certificate does not name `UBAG_HELPER_NODE_ID`; its adapter registry cannot be loaded.
  - `registry.go`: `RegistryDigest(dir)`: canonical SHA-256 over `registry.json` and every manifest it lists (sorted keys, numbers untouched, so CRLF and formatting never change it; a missing or malformed file, a path escape or a duplicate id is an error). Both sides call this function; the primary compares it with its own `adapters/`.
  - `hoststats.go`: `CgroupSampler` reports the node's own cgroup v2 budget (memory working set against `memory.max`, CPU rate against `cpu.max`), ADR-0009. Anything unreadable is an error, which the service reports as **zero totals** (the primary's `SampleFromCapacity` turns that into worst-case pressure), never as an idle node.
- **Moved**: the P4.10 asset staging client is now `internal/helper/staging` (package `staging`): it links the artifact store, `helperapi` and `jobs` (MinIO and Postgres drivers), which the service core must not. Nothing imported it yet; its tests moved unchanged.

## Import weight (the spec's "measure before extracting a runner subpackage")

Measured with `go list -deps` on this tree (non-standard-library packages):

| Package | Packages | Third-party pulled in |
|---|---|---|
| `internal/executor` | 228 | grpc 64, protobuf 33, x/ 32, **wazero 31**, **MinIO 14**, **pgx 7**, **NATS 6**, plus alerts, plugins, topology, siem, audit, conversations, antigravity, jobs ... |
| `internal/helper` before this slice (staging only) | 176 | grpc, protobuf, MinIO 14, pgx 7 (via artifacts / helperapi / helperauth / nodes) |
| `cmd/ubag-helper` (this slice) | 114 | grpc 64, protobuf 33, x/ 13, the proto module: nothing else (binary 17.8 MB on Windows) |

Conclusion: importing `internal/executor` would double the helper's package count and drag the plugin runtime, the object-store and queue clients and the Postgres driver into a binary that must hold none of them. Justified, so the service core never imports it: the runner is an interface here, and **P4.12 extracts the real runner (ProcessWorkerRunner / DaemonPool plus the worker env allowlist) into its own package** instead of importing the executor. `imports_test.go` fails if the core or the binary ever links anything outside gRPC, protobuf, x/ and the proto module.

## Env (the helper's whole configuration; none is a secret, key material is a file)

`UBAG_HELPER_LISTEN` (WireGuard IP:port), `UBAG_HELPER_NODE_ID`, `UBAG_HELPER_CA_FILE` (manager CA that issues the primary's certificate), `UBAG_HELPER_TLS_CERT_FILE` / `UBAG_HELPER_TLS_KEY_FILE` (this node's certificate), `UBAG_HELPER_PRIMARY_URI_SAN` (`spiffe://ubag/primary/<id>`), `UBAG_HELPER_PRIMARY_SPKI_SHA256` (optional, comma separated, at most two), `UBAG_HELPER_WORKLOAD_VERSION`, `UBAG_HELPER_ADAPTERS_DIR` (default `./adapters`), `UBAG_HELPER_CHROME_BIN` (optional), `UBAG_HELPER_MAX_ATTEMPTS` (1..8, default 1). The CA / cert / key names are the ones the primary's listener uses (P4.5), with the matching meaning on each side; ALLOCATION.md records them.

## Deviations

- The proto gained `registry_digest` (two additive fields): the spec asks the helper to report it and the contract had no field for it. It is not in `features` strings because the primary compares it, and a typed field can be checked, golden-pinned and linted.
- `staging.go` / `staging_test.go` moved from `internal/helper` to `internal/helper/staging` (a P4.10 file) so the service core stays dependency-light; the P4.10 shard is annotated.
- No `assets.go`, and `pool.go` is admission bookkeeping, not a process pool: P4.10 chose the pull model (the helper fetches under attempt tokens), so `StageManifest` is not served, and the process pool is the P3.6 `DaemonPool` that P4.12 wires behind `Runner`.
- The runner was not extracted from the executor here: measured (above), justified, and deferred to P4.12 where the real runner lands; the `Runner` interface is the seam.
- A new identity convention: the primary's certificate must carry the URI SAN `spiffe://ubag/primary/<id>` (the contract and ADR-0010 only defined the node side). It is an interface assumption to confirm with the manager team.
- A re-attach by repeating `RunAttempt` (replay from sequence 1) instead of a new RPC or a `resume_after` field: it needs no further contract change and the primary's `HelperIngest` already replays idempotently. Several streams may be attached to one attempt at once (bounded by the 32-stream limit); each replays the same log.
- `KeyPair` is duplicated from `helperauth` (with an own-certificate check hook) because `helperauth` imports the node registry and with it the Postgres driver.
- ADR number: 0013 (ADR-0012 landed with P5.5).
- Test certificates only (in-memory CA via `helperauth/authtest`); no dev tooling issues a primary certificate yet.

## Follow-ups

- **P4.12**: extract the runner into its own package, implement `Runner` over the `DaemonPool` (worker JSONL events to `AttemptEventType`, `identity_ref` to the P4.16 profile directory, `helperWorkerEnv`, `staging.ReadOnlyStore` + `MaterializeAttachments`), start at one workload, and pass `UBAG_HELPER_CHROME_BIN` on; set the manual-action semantics for `session.manual_action_required`.
- **Dial slice (primary client)**: present a certificate with the URI SAN `spiffe://ubag/primary/<id>` from the manager CA; verify the helper's certificate (its node URI SAN and the registry SPKI pin); keepalive no more often than every 10 s; measure clock skew from `HandshakeResponse.server_time` (the helper expires leases on its own clock against the primary's absolute `lease_expires_at`) and refuse a drifting node; compare `registry_digest` (Handshake and every capacity report) with `helper.RegistryDigest` of the primary's `adapters/` and make a mismatch ineligible; call `Drain` every tick while a grant says draining/revoked; on `InspectAttempt` `UNKNOWN` reconcile from the ledger and never re-run an attempt it shows as submitted; re-attach with `RunAttempt` after a broken stream and treat `Aborted` as a lost lease.
- Dev tooling: `deploy/mtls/gen-certs.{sh,ps1} --primary` to issue the primary's client certificate (the `--node` mode already issues the helper's).
- Packaging and deployment (Dockerfile / unit file, Docker limits, certificate issuance, the WireGuard bind) are external and not in this repo.
- Voice (P5.7): a voice attempt must not be cancelled by the drain grace (ADR-0009); nothing in this service knows about voice yet.
- A crash-safe journal would turn `UNKNOWN` after a restart into a real answer; not needed while the primary reconciles from its ledger, revisit if reconcile windows hurt.
- The checked-in `packages/proto/gen/go/ubag/v1/jobs.pb.go` and `jobs_grpc.pb.go` are behind `jobs.proto`: regenerating with the pinned plugins changes them. Left untouched (outside this slice); whoever owns `jobs.proto` should regenerate.
