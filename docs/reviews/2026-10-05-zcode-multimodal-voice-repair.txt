PRIORITY 4: CLEANUP, EXPIRY, FENCING, AND RECONNECT (VERIFIED DEFECTS)
Sources: serve.go:180-196; media.go:250-259,297-322; voice_handlers.go:467-497,535-570; SQL/memory RenewLease and SweepExpired.

The sweeper releases database leases without disconnecting audio or stopping the provider, allowing an old media connection to survive resource reassignment. Media failure/closure does not terminate the store record. Shutdown closeVoice closures do not close the MediaHub. RenewLease accepts already-expired sessions before the sweeper runs (reproduced). The SQLite sweeper selects expired IDs, then updates without rechecking expiry, so a concurrent renewal can be terminated. The handler ignores renewal errors after negotiation. Reconnect is documented as permitted, but MediaHub rejects an existing media connection. A stale closeSession callback deletes by session ID without confirming the mapped mediaSession is the same generation.

Make expiry, owner loss, disconnect, errors, explicit termination, and shutdown converge on idempotent teardown. Fence stale owners before resource reuse. Make expired-lease renewal fail atomically; make sweeps recheck expiry and status atomically. Route cleanup to the owning replica and guarantee stale media cannot remain active even if that replica is unavailable. Implement controlled reconnect replacement/ICE restart and protect replacement connections from stale callbacks. Do not ignore failed store operations.
Acceptance: worker/replica crash or expiry stops audio before another appointment uses the environment; renew-versus-sweep races are safe; expired leases cannot revive; reconnect works; terminating via another gateway replica cleans up the real media owner.

PRIORITY 6: HARDEN THE AUDIO RELAY (VERIFIED CLEANUP FAILURE + STATIC DEFECTS)
Sources: audio-relay.py:183-305; opus_bridge.py close methods.

stop is initialized after decoder/encoder construction, but finally always calls stop.set(). Constructor failure therefore masks the cause with UnboundLocalError and skips connection cleanup (reproduced). Pump threads join without deadlines. Normal EOF/return does not consistently stop the other pump, which may block on socket, FIFO, or monitor reads. Encoder/decoder handles are never closed. Device readiness checks only microphone presence and stops monitoring after first success, even if PulseAudio restarts. Decode assumes a 20ms maximum, while legal Opus packets can represent longer durations; incoming codec negotiation and packet timing are not explicitly constrained or handled.

Initialize cleanup state before risky operations, use deterministic finally cleanup for sockets/processes/native handles, signal both pumps on every exit, and unblock/join them with deadlines. Make device health and recovery cover microphone AND speaker monitor. Bound active sessions, stalled peers, and buffers. Explicitly negotiate/validate Opus and either correctly handle legal packet durations and RTP ordering or enforce/document a compatible constraint.
Acceptance: decoder/encoder failures, EOF, slow peers, corrupt frames, monitor exit, Pulse restart, and disconnect leave no sockets, subprocesses, native handles, or blocked session threads behind.
